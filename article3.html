California Sinks into the Pacific

Just 2 hours ago the US faced an incredible loss. The edge of California has started to submerge into the Pacific ocean. Reports by meteorologist to evacuate started a day prior. Meteorologists predicted we’d loss up to 6 miles of land across the entire shoreline, and police started evacuating homes close to sea. Actual measurements show anything from 15-30 miles of actual loss. But why is this happening? It’s quite simple, global warming. As the planet average temperature is rising and snow caps are melting the water levels are rising and weather conditions are getting worse. When a big tsunami hit the entirety of California the water did was expected not to retreat all the way and boy were they correct. Luckily casualties were relatively low for such an unprecedented event.
